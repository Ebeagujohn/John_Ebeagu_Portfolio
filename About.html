My Story

From Questions to code

My academic journey began with Philosophy — a discipline that trains you to ask harder questions, question hidden assumptions, and reason from first principles. That foundation has shaped everything I do.

As AI became the defining technology of our era, I realized my philosophical training wasn't a detour — it was exactly the lens the field needed. I began researching AI ethics seriously, contributing academic work on autonomy, rational sovereignty, and the philosophical dimensions of human-AI relationships.

Today, I'm pairing that ethical foundation with technical skill. I'm enrolled in a fullstack web development program at HiiT PLC, learning to build the systems I want to see governed well.

My long-term goal is AI engineering with a human-centered focus — building systems that augment human capability without undermining human dignity.
